# ADR-0001: Record architecture decisions

**Status:** Accepted (engineering) — engineering decision, not institutional approval.
**Date:** 2026-10-15

## Context
The prompt requires every Category-B (derived) decision to be recorded.

## Decision
Use lightweight ADRs (context / decision / consequences / source links) in `docs/adr/NNNN-title.md`. ADRs are engineering decisions; they never stand in for institutional approval.

## Consequences
Reviewers can trace why a design exists. Open business questions go to `docs/decisions/open-decisions.md`, not ADRs.
